Our Mission

Help people move better
so they can live better.

Range & Motion was founded on a simple belief: movement is freedom. When your body moves well, you live more fully — more actively, more independently, more joyfully. We exist to help people of every age and ability reclaim and expand that freedom.

We are not a fitness studio. We are not a physical therapy clinic. We are something new — a movement intelligence practice that combines expert human care, intelligent technology, and a deeply personal approach to help you move through every stage of life.

Our Difference

Built on four foundations.

Expert Care

Skilled practitioners. Personalized attention. Proven outcomes. Every session is tailored to you — never a protocol off a shelf.

Intelligent Systems

MIOS powers every client relationship — capturing movement data, tracking progress, and delivering insights that make every visit more effective.

Whole-Person Focus

Movement, recovery, and performance — considered together. Because your body doesn't operate in isolated parts, and neither do we.

Long-Term Partnership

We don't treat you today and send you on your way. We build a long-term picture of your movement and grow with you over years, not visits.

Every Visit

From the moment you arrive.

Your visit begins with a complimentary hibiscus tea — a simple gesture of welcome that sets the tone for everything that follows. Warm. Personal. Unhurried. This is a space where you are seen, understood, and cared for.
