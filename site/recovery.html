Recovery Philosophy

Recovery isn't optional.
It's part of the program.

Most people treat recovery as a reward — something you do after you've earned it. We treat it as essential. Your body does its best work when it has the right conditions to repair. Our recovery lounge is designed to give your body exactly what it needs.

Infrared Sauna

Deep heat. Deep relief.

Our premium cedar infrared sauna uses far-infrared technology to penetrate deeper than traditional saunas. The result: improved circulation, reduced muscle tension, detoxification, and a profound sense of calm. 30 minutes feels like a full reset.

  • Improved circulation
  • Muscle recovery
  • Detoxification
  • Stress relief
  • Better sleep

Cold Plunge

The reset your body craves.

Cold water immersion triggers a powerful physiological response — reducing inflammation, flushing metabolic waste, and flooding your system with endorphins. Used alone or in contrast with our sauna, it's one of the most effective recovery tools available.

  • Reduced inflammation
  • Accelerated recovery
  • Mood elevation
  • Mental clarity

Relaxation Space

A quiet retreat between sessions. Natural light, plants, and warm materials — designed for stillness. Your recovery begins the moment you walk through the door.

Hydration

Premium hydration to support your recovery. Electrolyte water, hibiscus tea, and recovery drinks available throughout your visit.

Contrast Therapy

Hot. Cold. Transformed.

The combination of infrared sauna and cold plunge — contrast therapy — is one of the most powerful recovery protocols available. Alternating heat and cold triggers circulation, reduces soreness, and accelerates healing in ways neither alone can match.

Infrared Sauna 15–20 min
Cold Plunge 2–5 min
Repeat 2–3 rounds
