Meet MIOS.

The Mobility Intelligence Operating System.

Technology that knows your movement — so your practitioner always knows exactly where you are and where you can go.

Movement Intelligence

Your movement, measured.
Your progress, visible.

Most wellness experiences end when you leave. MIOS changes that. Every session, every assessment, every improvement is captured and connected — building an intelligent picture of how your body moves over time. Your practitioner sees your full history at every visit. You see your progress clearly. Together, you move forward with precision.

What MIOS Does

Six capabilities.
One system.

Movement Assessment

Every client begins with a comprehensive movement screening — establishing your personal baseline and identifying opportunities for improvement.

Progress Tracking

Every session is recorded. Every improvement is measured. You always know exactly how far you've come.

Mobility Scoring

Your movement translated into a clear, understandable score — so you can see your progress and benchmark against your own history.

Personalized Recommendations

MIOS analyzes your patterns and generates individualized recommendations for sessions, home exercises, and recovery priorities.

Home Exercise Programs

Your personalized home program lives in MIOS — accessible anytime, updated as you progress, designed to extend your in-clinic work.

Appointment History & Milestones

Every visit logged. Every milestone celebrated. A complete record of your movement journey from first assessment forward.

Our Commitment

Technology supports the practitioner.
It never replaces the human relationship.

MIOS is a tool in the hands of an expert. It sharpens what great practitioners already do — it never substitutes for the judgment, intuition, and care of a skilled human being. Every recommendation is reviewed. Every protocol is personalized. The technology makes the relationship more powerful. Nothing more.

A Glimpse of MIOS

Your movement, beautifully visualized.

Your MIOS dashboard shows your mobility score, session history, home program, and progress over time — designed to be clear, motivating, and actionable. No medical jargon. No confusing charts. Just your movement story, told simply.

Client portal coming soon. When MIOS launches, every Range & Motion member will have full access to their movement dashboard, home program library, and appointment history.

Where It Begins

Your assessment is your first data point.

Your MIOS profile begins the moment you book your complimentary movement assessment. Everything we learn about how you move becomes the foundation — the baseline against which every improvement is measured. Your journey starts here.
